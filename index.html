Our mission

NextGen HERoes empowers girls to explore, build, and lead in STEM. We create supportive spaces to practice hard skills, celebrate effort, and make professional pathways feel real and reachable.

Confidence → Action

Weekly study circles and project sprints that turn curiosity into real builds.

Community → Belonging

Peer mentorship so no one is learning alone.

Career → Access

Guest speakers, job-shadowing, and portfolio-ready challenges.

Focus areas

  • STEM Study Circles: problem-solving sessions across CS, math, physics, and chem.
  • Mini-Challenges: 1-week build sprints with showcases and prizes.
  • STEM & Gender Gap Talks: evidence-based workshops.
  • Social Campaigns: share wins, role models, and opportunities.

Legal status

NextGen HERoes Foundation is incorporated as a nonprofit society in British Columbia under the Societies Act (Society No. S0083843). Incorporation does not automatically mean donations are tax-deductible; tax receipts require registered charity status.

FAQ

Are you an official nonprofit?

Yes. NextGen HERoes Foundation is incorporated as a nonprofit society in British Columbia under the Societies Act (Society No. S0083843), incorporated November 13, 2025. Incorporation does not automatically make donations tax-deductible; tax receipts require registered charity status.

How much does it cost to join?

Free. We’re committed to keeping access open; optional merch or event fees may apply for special activities.

Do I need prior experience?

Nope. Curiosity is enough. We meet you where you are.
